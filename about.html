Our philosophy

The story

Pivot Square,N1 City

Exclusive Auto Group opened in 2014 with a simple idea: buying a special car should feel special. Owner Keegan Matthews built the business around a hand-picked floor, honest advice and a showroom you'd want to spend time in.

Today the floor runs from Hilux Legends and family SUVs to Lamborghini, McLaren and Porsche, with every trade-in welcome and delivery anywhere in South Africa.
